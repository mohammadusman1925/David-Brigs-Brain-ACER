# General Instruction

The governance layer for every brand in this portfolio. Read this first, before any other
document.

---

## 1. The architecture

This is not a tier stack where one document overrides the next. It is a mentor plus a running
order.

**David is the mentor.** `mentor/david-brain.md` is the judgment underneath every decision. He
scaled the businesses this system came from, so his thinking is what is being applied. He is not
a step — he is present at all of them, and he decides what else gets used. Every creative call
names the module it came from. A decision that cannot be traced back to David is not made.

**Metabolae is a reference, not a fixed strategy.** `reference/metabolae/` is a proven system and
the closest thing here to a worked example of the thinking, so it is what we look at first and
what we suggest adopting. It is not a pipeline that runs by default. When part of its method
fits the job, propose it by name and say why. When it does not, say so and do something else.
Never force a brand through it just because it exists.

**Module 1.1 is where output gets framed and named.** `system/master-concept.md` — use case,
angle, awareness, bucket, format, asset name.

**Evolve and anything like it are suggested.** Applied when the format calls for them. Evolve is
video scripts only.

A typical iteration: look at how Metabolae does it, frame it through Module 1.1, reach for
Evolve if the output is a video. That is a default worth suggesting, not a rule.

### Where things live

## 5. Awareness — one map, three vocabularies

Three systems are in play. David's five Schwartz levels are canonical. The others are views onto
it.

| Schwartz (Module 9) | Module 1.1 | Metabolae |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Unaware | TOF | out of scope, deliberate |
| 2 Problem Aware | TOF | Stage 2 |
| 3 Solution Aware | MOF | Stage 3 |
| 4 Product Aware | MOF | Stage 4 |
| 5 Most Aware | BOF | Stage 5 |

Module 1.1 default weights outside promo periods: TOF 70, MOF 20, BOF 10. During a marketing
moment, shift toward BOF.

**Metabolae works Stage 2 and up.** Unaware is not targeted, and that is a deliberate choice,
not a hole to fix. The Protocol's numbering starts at Stage 2 for that reason, its bucket-size
override extends down to Stage 2 and stops there, and Evolve independently caps at Problem-Aware
or Product-Aware. Unaware work is Module 9 and Module 18 territory — story-led education.

Awareness also decides the funnel. Unaware and problem-aware need an advertorial or VSL before
the product page. Product-aware and most-aware can go straight there, and dragging them through
education just moves the finish line further away.

## 6. Stop and wait

Confirm the deliverable in plain terms before starting.

1. Lock use case and angle. **Stop.**
2. Confirm awareness stage. **Stop.**
3. Present hooks. **Stop.** Wait for a pick before writing a body.

If past work exists for the brand, read what worked and what failed before writing anything new.
Never skip a gate to save a turn — the judgment at the gate is the point.

## 7. The project wall

One brand's facts never enter another brand's work. Load one project card at a time. No fact,
angle, mechanism, proof point, or product spec crosses between brands, for any reason.

This is a wall on facts, not on thinking. Method is shared by design.

Brands: heykeratin.com · garden-trend.store · sleeppods.se · norillo.se · Metabolae · Heart
Health Co. All separate clients. sleeppods.se and norillo.se sit under the same owner but are
still separate brands.

All output in English unless specified. For the Swedish brands, research and strategy in
English; final ad copy in Swedish when asked.

## 8. Congruence

Before writing for a brand, read `projects/<brand>/house-style.md`, then enough of its `outputs/`
to hear the voice, then its `research/` for the customer's own words.

Approved output is what new work has to read like — voice, structure, rhythm, length. When a
draft drifts from it, say where and why rather than shipping it. Empty folders are normal at
first. Say so rather than inventing a house style.

## 9. Standing rules

- **Research before writing.** 80% research, 20% writing. Never start without knowing awareness
  level, sophistication stage, real desire, and blocking belief.
- **Rule of One.** One desire, one argument, one CTA.
- **Copy cannot create desire.** It channels desire that already exists onto a product.
- **Specificity beats vagueness.** Numbers over feelings. Concrete over abstract.
- **Sixth grade reading level.** Contractions, varied sentence length, one idea per sentence.
  Read it aloud — if it sounds stiff, rewrite it.
- **AI never leads.** It executes inside a framework the expert provides. Generic prompting
  produces generic output.
- **Never categorize.** Own a new category through the mechanism instead of comparing to an
  existing one.
- **Show, do not tell.** Behavioral change over benefit lists.
- **The hook is 80% of the value.** Spend effort accordingly.
- **Every claim needs a reason why.** A claim without one is hope marketing.
- **Exhaustion is per bucket, not per angle.** When a use case times angle wins in one bucket,
  run it across all four before building a new concept.
- **Scripts:** no bold text, no double quotation marks, no compliance language, disclaimers, or
  hedging. Legal handles compliance.
- **Deliverables are markdown files in the repo.** Not HTML tools that generate prompts to paste
  into a chat.
