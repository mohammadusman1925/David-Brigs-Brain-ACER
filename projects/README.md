# Projects

One folder per brand. Everything for that brand lives inside it. Nothing crosses between them.

```text
projects/<brand>/
  brand.md        the facts. product, offer, ICP, angles, objections, claims off-limits
  house-style.md  what new work has to read like. written from outputs/, never invented
  outputs/        approved and shipped work. the congruence source
  research/       reviews, CS logs, competitor pulls, avatar work, market notes
  briefs/         production briefs, current and past
```

`_template/` is the blank set. Copy the whole folder to start a brand.

## Before writing for a brand

1. Read `brand.md` for the facts. Never guess a product fact — an empty field is a gap to raise,
   not a blank to fill in.
2. Read `house-style.md` and enough of `outputs/` to hear the voice.
3. Read `research/` for the customer's own words. Their phrasing is the asset. Do not paraphrase
   it into marketing language.

Load one brand at a time. No fact, angle, mechanism, proof point or product spec crosses between
brands, for any reason.
